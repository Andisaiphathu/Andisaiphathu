## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🇿🇦 MzansiBiz — Full-Stack Business Platform

**MzansiBiz** is a full-stack project that we are developing **as a team** to create a digital platform that helps South African small and informal businesses establish an online presence, connect with customers, and access useful business and government-related information.

The project combines **frontend, backend, database, API integration, and user-focused features**, giving us practical experience working together on a real-world software system.

**Current areas of development include:**

* 💻 **Frontend:** Building the user interface and dashboards for different types of users
* ⚙️ **Backend:** Developing REST APIs and business logic using **Java and Spring Boot**
* 🗄️ **Database:** Designing and managing data for businesses, products, services, users and other platform features
* 🔐 **Authentication & Authorisation:** Supporting different user roles and access to platform features
* 🏪 **Business Management:** Allowing business owners to create and manage their business profiles, products and services
* 📍 **Business Discovery:** Helping customers discover local businesses and services
* 📊 **Business Dashboard:** Providing business owners with tools to manage their business and view useful information
* 🌾 **Agriculture Hub:** Providing information and resources relevant to farmers and agricultural businesses
* 🏛️ **Government Services:** Providing guidance and information around business formalisation and government services
* 🤝 **Team Collaboration:** Working together using Git/GitHub, dividing tasks, reviewing code and integrating different parts of the application

> 🚧 **Status:** Currently under active development as a team project.
